1. ABSTRACT
================================================================================

  S-MAS is a high-fidelity satellite operational digital twin that uses
  Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning (MARL) to autonomously optimize the
  orbital lifetime of a Low Earth Orbit (LEO) microsatellite. The system
  models the ESA PROBA-1 spacecraft (NORAD 26957) at ~600 km altitude,
  integrating real historical space weather data (2001-2020), radiation
  hazard maps, and a validated atmospheric drag model.

  The platform spans four programming languages across an eight-step pipeline:
  C++ (high-fidelity physics engine with fuel, thermal, ADCS, J2-J6 gravity,
  third-body perturbations, penumbra eclipse, and link-budget comms), Python
  (MARL training with MAPPO), C# (operational controller with ONNX inference,
  watchdog timer, and 4-satellite constellation support), and TypeScript/React
  (WebGPU 3D dashboard with full telemetry visualization).

  Three heterogeneous AI agents (Navigation, Bus Management, Mission) coordinate
  under a rule-based FDIR safety governor using the MAPPO algorithm with
  Centralized Training and Decentralized Execution (CTDE).

  The physics engine features:
    - High-fidelity gravity model (J2-J6 zonal harmonics + Moon/Sun third-body)
    - Improved NRLMSISE-00 atmospheric model with F10.7 81-day average and
      Dst storm-time heating
    - Quaternion-based ADCS with 3-axis reaction wheels and sub-stepping
    - Conical shadow eclipse model with smooth penumbra transitions
    - Link-budget communication model with SNR-based visibility
    - Finite propellant (Tsiolkovsky) and 3-node lumped thermal model

  The physics engine was validated against 30 days of real PROBA-1 TLE data
  using SGP4 propagation, achieving an RMSE of ~18.3 km after calibration.
  100-seed Monte Carlo V&V and 6 worst-case scenario tests confirm robust
  survival across diverse operational conditions.

  Post-deployment analysis identified and resolved five critical failure modes,
  extending operational lifetime from 442 days to 13+ years (genuine battery
  end-of-life). The system successfully survived a full 10-year simulation
  window with healthy subsystems.

4. C++ PHYSICS ENGINE (Phases 1 + A + B + C)
================================================================================

  4.1 Orbital Mechanics (upgraded Phase C — Step 8)

    - 4th-order Runge-Kutta (RK4) integrator at dt = 5.0 s
    - Earth gravity: HIGH-FIDELITY J2-J6 zonal harmonics
        J2 =  1.0826267e-3   (dominant oblateness term)
        J3 = -2.5324859e-6   (pear-shaped asymmetry)
        J4 = -1.61962159e-6  (higher-order oblateness)
        J5 = -2.2771610e-7   (tesseral contribution)
        J6 =  5.40665e-7     (fine correction)
      Implementation: Legendre polynomial evaluation P_n(q) and P'_n(q)
      for n=2..6, accumulated in Cartesian coordinates.
    - Third-body perturbations (Moon & Sun — Step 8):
        Sun:  GM_sun  = 1.32712440018e20 m³/s²
              Position via approximate_sun_direction() × 1 AU
        Moon: GM_moon = 4.9027779e12 m³/s²
              Analytical low-precision ephemeris (L, M, F polynomials)
        Formula: a_3b = GM * ((r_body - r_sat)/|r_body - r_sat|³ - r_body/|r_body|³)
    - Aerodynamic drag: F_D = -0.5 × ρ × Cd × A × v² × v̂
    - Atmosphere co-rotation correction: v_rel = v - (ω × r)
    - Thrust: continuous 3D vector with throttle [0, 1]
    - 5% throttle deadband applied to prevent micro-thrust jitter
    - Dynamic mass: current_mass = SAT_MASS_KG - (SAT_FUEL_KG - fuel_kg)

  4.2 NRLMSISE-00 Atmospheric Model (improved Phase C — Step 8)

    - Exospheric temperature (upgraded with Dst storm-time heating):
        T_inf = 500.0 + 3.5 × F10.7a + 1.5 × (F10.7 - F10.7a) + 1.5 × Ap
                - 1.5 × min(0, Dst)
      where:
        F10.7a = 81-day centered moving average (pre-computed, 1944-hour window)
        Dst = Disturbance Storm Time index (from space_weather.csv)
    - Bates-Walker temperature profile: T(h) = T_inf - (T_inf - T_120) × exp(-σ × dh)
    - Diffusive equilibrium density with diurnal and latitudinal modulation
    - density_multiplier = 0.01 (calibrated for multi-year orbital lifetime)
      Short-term validation (30 days): 0.1 gives RMSE ~18.3 km
      Long-term realism (multi-year): 0.01 for realistic ~20+ yr decay at 600km

  4.3 Propulsion Subsystem (Phase A)

    Tsiolkovsky Rocket Equation:
      delta_m = m × (1 - exp(-dv / (Isp × g0)))
      Achievable dv = Isp × g0 × ln(m_before / m_after)

    Parameters:
      SAT_FUEL_KG       = 5.6 kg (hydrazine, PROBA-1)
      THRUSTER_ISP_S    = 220 s (monopropellant)
      G0                = 9.80665 m/s²
      FUEL_CRITICAL_KG  = 0.1 kg
      Max delta-v       = ~132 m/s (full fuel budget)

    Behavior:
      - Fuel tracked per-step, depleted by Tsiolkovsky equation
      - Thrust disabled when fuel_kg <= 0
      - Partial burns supported (fuel runs out mid-step)
      - FUEL_DEPLETED_LOW done reason (fuel=0 + alt<400km)

  4.4 Thermal Subsystem (Phase A)

    3-Node Lumped Thermal Model:
      Nodes: Bus (50kg, 900 J/kg/K), Battery, Payload
      Inter-node conduction: Bus<->Battery 2 W/K, Bus<->Payload 1 W/K

    Heat Sources:
      - Solar flux: 1361 W/m² × absorptivity(0.3) × area × cos(sun_angle)
      - Earth albedo: 0.3 × solar_flux × view_factor
      - Internal dissipation: power_draw_w (bus electrical waste heat)

    Heat Sinks:
      - Stefan-Boltzmann radiative cooling to space (2.7 K)
      - Radiator area: 0.25 m², emissivity: 0.8

    Battery Heater:
      - Power: 7 W
      - Activation: temp_battery < -5°C
      - Hysteresis: 5°C (deactivates at 0°C)
      - Draws directly from battery SoC

    Battery Temperature Factor:
      - battery_temp_factor = 1.0 in [0°C, 40°C]
      - Linear degradation outside: min 0.5 at extreme temps
      - Modulates effective battery capacity: cap_eff = cap × factor

  4.5 Attitude Dynamics & Control System — ADCS (Step 4)

    Quaternion-Based Attitude Representation:
      State vector: q = [q0, q1, q2, q3] (scalar-first convention)
      Angular velocity: ω = [ωx, ωy, ωz] (body frame, rad/s)
      Quaternion kinematics: q̇ = 0.5 × q ⊗ [0, ωx, ωy, ωz]
      Integration: q_new = normalize(q + q̇ × dt_sub)

    3-Axis Reaction Wheel Model:
      - Wheel angular momentum: h = [hx, hy, hz] (Nms)
      - Saturation limit: H_MAX = 0.2 Nms (micro-satellite class)
      - Torque command from AI agent → wheel acceleration
      - Desaturation: automatic when |h| approaches H_MAX

    External Torques:
      - Gravity gradient: T_gg ≈ 3μ/r³ × (I_z - I_y) × sin(2θ)/2
        Magnitude: ~10⁻⁷ Nm for PROBA-1 (small but cumulative)
      - Solar radiation pressure (SRP): computed from sun_dir and
        satellite cross-section

    Derived Quantities:
      - sun_angle: angle between panel normal and sun direction (rad)
        Solar power = SAT_SOLAR_POWER_W × panel_eff × cos(sun_angle)
        (replaces the approximate binary eclipse-based model)
      - nadir_error: pointing error from nadir (rad)
        CHRIS imaging requires nadir_error < 5° (0.087 rad)

    Sub-Stepping (dt=5s Resolution):
      - Attitude control loop runs at 1 Hz (5 sub-steps per main step)
      - sub_dt = dt / 5 = 1.0 s per sub-step
      - External torques computed once per main step
      - Quaternion integration and wheel update per sub-step
      - AI decisions at 5s granularity, physics at 1s for stability

  4.6 Conical Shadow / Eclipse Model (Step 7 — Phase B)

    Replaces cylindrical shadow model with conical shadow geometry:

    Penumbra Factor Calculation:
      - Full sunlight:  factor = 1.0 (no shadow)
      - Penumbra:       factor ∈ (0.0, 1.0) — smooth transition
      - Umbra:          factor = 0.0 (fully shadowed)

    Physics:
      - Uses Sun/Earth angular radii from satellite position
      - Accounts for Earth J2 oblateness on shadow boundary
      - Smooth power transition over ~20 seconds:
        solar_w = SAT_SOLAR_POWER_W × panel_eff × cos_sun_angle × penumbra_factor

    Benefits:
      - Eliminates abrupt power ON/OFF transitions
      - More realistic battery charge/discharge cycling
      - Better thermal model input (gradual heating/cooling)

  4.7 Link-Budget Communication Model (Step 7 — Phase B)

    Replaces simple line-of-sight model with full link budget:

    Path Loss: L_fs = (4π × d × f / c)²
      where d = slant range (m), f = S-band frequency (Hz)

    Link Budget Components:
      - Transmitter power: P_tx (satellite S-band)
      - Antenna gains: G_tx (satellite), G_rx (ground station)
      - System noise temperature: T_sys
      - SNR calculation: SNR = P_tx + G_tx + G_rx - L_fs - kT_sys

    Visibility Decision:
      - gs_visible set only when SNR > threshold (not just elevation angle)
      - More realistic pass windows and contact durations

    Data Buffer & Downlink:
      - Onboard flash storage: 256 MB buffer (data_buffer_mb field)
      - CHRIS images queued for downlink during ground station passes
      - S-band constraint: 1 Mbps downlink bandwidth
      - snr_db field: strongest station SNR, or -999.0 when no LOS

  4.8 Power & Battery Subsystem

    - GaAs solar arrays: 90 W BOL, degraded by panel_efficiency
    - Solar power now modulated by cos(sun_angle) from ADCS
    - Li-ion battery: 360,000 J (100 Wh) BOL
    - Power modes:
        Normal:     30W bus + 25W payload = 55W
        Deep sleep:  5W bus only
        Payload ON:  25W additional draw
    - Arrhenius cycle degradation (calibrated):
        BATT_CYCLE_DEGRAD = 0.00002 (2.5x slower than original)
        BATT_THERMAL_FACT = 0.00005 (20x slower acceleration)
        Battery EOL (80%): ~Year 14 (matches real satellite data)
    - Panel drift (calibrated):
        PANEL_DRIFT_SIGMA = 0.00003/step (3.3x slower random walk)
        Secular loss rate: 2e-9/step (5x slower, ~1.5%/yr realistic)
        Floor: 0.30

  4.9 Stochastic Layer

    - Sensor noise: pos sigma=50m, vel sigma=0.5m/s, SoC sigma=1%
    - Actuator model: 1-3 step action delay queue, +/-5% error
    - Epistemic drift: Cd random walk (sigma=0.001/step)
    - SEU generator:
        Base probability: 0.001
        SAA multiplier: 100x
        Fatal rate: 0.0001 of base (100x reduction from original)
        ~2% chance of fatal SEU over 20 years

  4.10 FDIR State Machine

    State      | Trigger    | AI Control
    -----------|------------|--------------------------------------------
    NOMINAL    | SoC > 20%  | Full autonomy
    DEGRADED   | SoC < 20%  | Payload OFF, thrust capped at 30%
    SAFE       | SoC < 10%  | All overridden, deep sleep forced
    RECOVERY   | SoC >= 15% | Payload OFF, transitioning back to NOMINAL

    Transition hysteresis:
      DEGRADED -> RECOVERY: SoC >= 25%
      SAFE -> RECOVERY:     SoC >= 15%
      RECOVERY -> NOMINAL:  SoC >= 30%

5. MARL FRAMEWORK (Phase 2)
================================================================================

  5.1 Algorithm: MAPPO (Multi-Agent PPO)

    - Shared policy architecture (all agents share trunk weights)
    - Centralized Training, Decentralized Execution (CTDE)
    - GAE (lambda = 0.95, gamma = 0.99) for advantage estimation
    - Clip ratio: 0.2, entropy coefficient: 0.01
    - Learning rate: 3e-4, batch size: 4096
    - 16 parallel environments during training (num_envs = 16)
    - Episode length: 362,880 steps (~3 weeks simulated time)
    - Rollout length: 1,176 steps (~1 orbit period)
    - PPO update epochs: 2

  5.2 Network Architecture

    SharedActorCritic:
      Trunk: MLP(obs_dim=42, hidden=128, layers=2, activation=tanh)
      NavigationHead: Linear(128, 8) -> mu[4] + log_std[4] -> Gaussian + tanh
      ResourceHead:   Linear(128, 1) -> Bernoulli (deep_sleep)
      MissionHead:    Linear(128, 1) -> Bernoulli (payload_on)
      Critic:         Linear(128, 1) -> V(s) scalar

    Total parameters: ~22,500

    Quantization Support (Step 5):
      - FP32: default, full precision
      - FP16: via onnxconverter_common float16 conversion
      - INT8: via onnxruntime.quantization (dynamic quantization)

  5.5 Reward Function (9 Components — Survival)

    R_survival = w_alive
              - w_fuel × thrust_mag × throttle × fuel_scale
              - w_fuel_critical × (fuel < 10%)
              + w_coast_bonus × (throttle < 0.01)
              - w_dod × (1 - SoC)
              - w_fdir × FDIR_transition
              - w_fatal × terminal_failure
              - w_alt × max(0, |alt - target| - deadband)
              - w_thermal × temp_deviation

    Weight            | Value     | Purpose
    ------------------|-----------|------------------------------------------
    w_alive           | 5.0       | Per-step survival bonus
    w_fuel            | 10.0      | Fuel consumption penalty (scales with 1/fuel_frac, capped 20x)
    w_fuel_critical   | 500.0     | Fuel below 10% penalty
    w_coast_bonus     | 2.0       | Reward for not thrusting (fuel conservation)
    w_dod             | 50.0      | Depth-of-Discharge penalty
    w_fdir            | 200.0     | FDIR NOM->DEG/SAFE transition penalty
    w_fatal           | 50,000.0  | Terminal failure penalty
    w_alt             | 0.3       | Altitude deviation from target (deadband 35 km)
    w_thermal         | 50.0      | Battery temp outside [-10, 45]°C

  5.6 Reward Function (7 Additional Components — Mission)

    R_mission = R_survival
              + w_valid_target × first_over_target
              + 2.0 × continued_imaging
              - w_saa_penalty × payload_in_saa
              - w_idle_power × payload_not_over_target
              - w_sloth_penalty × sleeping_over_target
              - 150.0 × low_battery_imaging
              - 50.0 × meta_override_penalty

    Weight            | Value     | Purpose
    ------------------|-----------|------------------------------------------
    w_valid_target    | 100.0     | First imaging step over valid target
    w_saa_penalty     | 200.0     | Payload ON inside SAA
    w_idle_power      | 15.0      | Payload ON but not over target
    w_sloth_penalty   | 50.0      | Deep sleep while SoC>90% over target

    Valid target criteria:
      NOT in_saa AND NOT in_eclipse AND lat in [-60, 60]° AND solar_power >= 10W

  5.7 Progressive Degradation Training

    At each episode reset, the engine starts at a random mission age:
      capacity_j    ~ Uniform(120,000 J, 360,000 J)
      panel_eff     ~ Uniform(0.45, 1.0)
      target_alt    ~ Uniform(580.6 km, 610.6 km)

    During the episode, hardware degrades progressively (orbit-by-orbit):
      panel_decay   = 0.0017 per orbit  (-> ~18% drop over 1 week)
      capacity_decay = 645 J per orbit  (-> ~67 kJ drop over 1 week)
      Floor: panel_eff >= 0.30, capacity_j >= 80,000 J


6. MISSION LAYER (Phase 3)
================================================================================

  6.1 Mission Agent (CHRIS Instrument Control)

    - Toggles optical payload ON/OFF based on orbital position
    - Power draw penalty when active (25W additional)
    - +100.0 reward for valid target imaging (one-shot per pass)
    - -200.0 penalty for payload ON inside SAA boundaries
    - -50.0 sloth penalty: sleeping while SoC>90% over valid target
    - Greed prevention: imaging below battery safety floor yields -150.0
    - Nadir pointing constraint: CHRIS requires nadir_error < 5° (ADCS)

  6.2 Meta-Coordination

    - If Bus agent activates deep_sleep, payload is forced OFF
    - FDIR governor overrides all agents during DEGRADED/SAFE modes
    - Eclipse conservation: deep sleep forced during ALL eclipse passages

  6.3 C# Power Safety Governor (Multi-Layer)

    Layer                | Trigger         | Action
    ---------------------|-----------------|--------------------------------
    Critical Power       | SoC < 30%       | Force deep sleep + payload off
    Warning Power        | SoC < 60%       | Force payload off
    Eclipse Conservation | in_eclipse == 1 | Force deep sleep + payload off
    FDIR Passthrough     | SAFE mode       | Full override (deep sleep)
    Watchdog Timeout     | Inference > 500ms | Force SAFE mode (Step 5)

    Eclipse Conservation reduces eclipse drain by 83%:
      Without: 30W × 2100s = 63,000 J per eclipse
      With:     5W × 2100s = 10,500 J per eclipse


7. C# CONTROLLER (Phase 4 + Steps 5-6)
================================================================================

  7.1 Architecture

    - .NET 10.0 console application
    - P/Invoke bindings to smas_engine.dll with [StructLayout(Pack=1)]
    - ONNX Runtime inference for 3 agent models (nav, bus, mission)
    - Async WebSocket binary server on ws://localhost:8765
    - Input tensor: [1, 42] float32 (was [1, 29] -> [1, 37] -> [1, 42])

  7.2 Watchdog Timer (Step 5)

    - Inference call wrapped in 500ms CancellationTokenSource
    - Timeout triggers FDIR transition to SAFE mode
    - Protects against inference stalls on constrained hardware
    - Models LEON3 (25 MHz SPARC) compute budget constraints

  7.3 4-Satellite Constellation (Step 6)

    - 4 independent PhysicsEngine instances (seeds 42-45)
    - Time offsets: 0, 24h, 48h, 72h (Walker-delta spacing)
    - Independent FDIR governors and observation builders per satellite
    - Telemetry header includes SatID (1 byte) for demultiplexing
    - Constellation status: "N/4 ALIVE", terminates when all satellites done

    Header format:
    [Version(1B) | SatID(1B) | Seq(4B) | PayloadLen(4B) | Payload(222B) | CRC32(4B)]

  7.4 Binary Telemetry Protocol

    - Circular buffer (32 frames) for backpressure management
    - Stochastic network impairment (delay, jitter, packet drops)
    - CSV logger with offline replay engine
    - Extended CSV columns: fuel_fraction, temp_bus, temp_battery,
      temp_payload, heater_on, target_alt_km, sun_angle, nadir_error

  7.5 Fast-Forward Mode (--skip N)

    Simulates all physics steps at full CPU speed, broadcasts only every
    Nth step to the dashboard. Enables multi-year simulations:

      --skip 100    100x speed (1 year in ~1 minute)
      --skip 1000   1000x speed (10 years in ~5 minutes)

    Step count reference:
      1 day = 17,280 | 1 year = 6,307,200 | 10 years = 63,072,000

  7.6 Observation Normalizer

    - Exact C# port of Python's observation.py
    - 42-dim normalized vector (MinMax, Robust, log10, one-hot)
    - Includes fuel, thermal, target altitude, ADCS features
    - ABI validated at startup: StatePacket=222B, ActionPacket=20B


8. WEBGPU DASHBOARD (Phase 5)
================================================================================

  8.1 Technology: Vite + React 18 + TypeScript

  8.2 Rendering Engine

    - Primary: WebGPU with WGSL shaders (instanced rendering)
    - Fallback: WebGL2 with GLSL shaders (automatic detection)
    - Wireframe Earth (bright cyan-blue) + FDIR-colored satellite dot
    - Coordinate transform: Lat/Lon/Alt -> ECEF (X, Y, Z)
    - 4-satellite constellation: color-coded dots per satellite

  8.3 Dynamic SAA Heatmap

    Both WebGPU and WebGL shaders compute geographic UV coordinates from
    the vertex's local position and the Earth rotation angle uniform, so
    the heatmap remains geo-referenced as Earth rotates:
      1. Vertex shader passes local position (vLocalPos) to fragment shader
      2. Fragment shader undoes Earth rotation: geoPos = rotateY(-angle) * localPos
      3. Computes geoLat/geoLon from geoPos
      4. Maps to UV: geoUV = ( (lon+pi)/(2*pi), 1-(lat+pi/2)/pi )
      5. Samples heatmap at geoUV — SAA stays over South Atlantic

  8.4 Telemetry Decoder (useTelemetry hook)

    - Zero-allocation DataView parser
    - Directly decodes binary WebSocket stream to React state
    - Parses 222B payload (v4 StatePacket)
    - SatID-based demultiplexing for 4-satellite constellation
    - Auto-reconnect WebSocket: retries every 2 seconds

  8.5 HUD Design

    - Deep-space glassmorphism theme (backdrop blur, dark gradients)
    - Dynamic FDIR state indicators with pulse animations
    - Mission Lifetime counter: displays elapsed time as Xy Xd Xh format
    - SATELLITE DECEASED indicator with cause (reentry/battery/comms/fuel/seu)
    - Real-time SoC, altitude, agent actions, environment status
    - FDIR-colored satellite: blue (nominal), green (payload ON),
      amber (degraded), red (safe mode)
    - Fuel gauge: circular ring with green/amber/red color coding
      and "DEPLETED" indicator when fuel = 0%
    - Thermal panel: 3 temperature bars (bus, battery, payload)
      with safe-zone highlighting and heater LED indicator
    - ADCS display: sun angle, nadir error, wheel momentum readouts
    - 2×2 constellation grid (4 satellite panels, color-coded)
    - Ground control presets: Solar Max, Halloween Storm, Fuel Critical,
      Cold Eclipse scenarios

    - Long-term realism (multi-year): multiplier = 0.01
      Matches real PROBA-1 behavior (24-year operational life at ~600 km).
      Set as the default for operational simulations.
